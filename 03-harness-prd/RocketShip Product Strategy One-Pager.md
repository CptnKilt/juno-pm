# RocketShip Strategy One-Pager

## Strategy Statement

RocketShip's strategy is to become the default enterprise platform for trusted, governed, and scalable data operations, enabling data teams to deliver reliable business outcomes at speed without sacrificing compliance, security, or control.

## Where RocketShip Is Today

RocketShip is a hypergrowth B2B SaaS platform serving enterprise data teams across analytics, data engineering, and governance. Customers rely on RocketShip to manage increasingly complex data pipelines, models, and downstream consumption in environments that are regulated, distributed, and business critical.

As adoption grows, RocketShip is transitioning from feature delivery to operating at an enterprise scale, where trust, reliability, and predictability are as important as innovation speed.

## Current Problems and Constraints

As RocketShip scales across larger enterprises, several structural challenges emerge:

- Enterprise onboarding is slow and inconsistent due to varied data stacks, security models, and governance requirements
- Data quality and reliability issues are costly and difficult to diagnose quickly across complex pipelines
- Governance, compliance, and audit requirements grow faster than engineering capacity
- High-touch support and solutions work limits scale and increases cost to serve
- Institutional knowledge is fragmented across teams, tools, and customers, creating repeated work and slower response times

These constraints risk slowing growth, increasing operational cost, and weakening enterprise trust if not addressed systematically.

## Desired Future State

RocketShip becomes the system of record for trusted data operations inside the enterprise.

What success looks like:

- Enterprises onboard faster with predictable paths to value
- Data reliability incidents are rare, detectable early, and resolved quickly
- Governance and compliance scale without slowing product delivery
- Customers trust RocketShip with mission-critical data workflows
- RocketShip expands naturally across teams as trust compounds

## Strategic Pillars

1. **Trust at Scale** — Reliability, observability, lineage, and governance are first-class platform capabilities, not add-ons.
2. **Fast Time-to-Value** — Standardized patterns, templates, and guided workflows reduce friction from day one.
3. **Enterprise-Readiness by Default** — Security, access control, auditability, and compliance are embedded into every workflow.
4. **Scalable Operations** — The platform reduces dependency on high-touch support through automation, self-service, and clear operational guardrails.

## Strategic Priority Framework (for Decision-Making)

The following priority levels define how RocketShip evaluates incoming customer feedback, internal signals, and requests. These priorities are used to guide product decisions, roadmap sequencing, and trade-off discussions.

**P0 — Trust, Reliability, and Compliance (Non-Negotiable)**
*Definition:* Work that protects or restores customer trust in RocketShip as a system of record for enterprise data operations.
*Includes:* data correctness, integrity, and reliability failures; security vulnerabilities, permission errors, or access control gaps; governance, audit, or compliance risks; issues that block enterprise onboarding, renewals, or mission-critical workflows.
*Explicitly excludes:* UX polish or cosmetic improvements; feature enhancements without trust or compliance implications; performance improvements that do not impact correctness, security, or auditability.

**P1 — Enterprise Time-to-Value and Adoption**
*Definition:* Work that materially reduces friction for enterprises to onboard, adopt, and expand RocketShip usage.
*Includes:* enterprise onboarding workflows and setup automation; guided experiences, templates, and documentation; reducing dependency on high-touch support during initial adoption.
*Explicitly excludes:* net-new product categories; advanced features used only by power users; internal platform work without clear customer adoption impact.

**P2 — Platform Leverage and Operational Scale**
*Definition:* Work that improves RocketShip's ability to scale efficiently by reducing operational overhead and internal complexity.
*Includes:* platform reliability and scalability improvements; internal automation and self-service tooling; reductions in support load, incident response time, or cost-to-serve.
*Explicitly excludes:* customer-specific one-off solutions; feature requests that do not improve operational efficiency or scale.

**P3 — Incremental Improvements and Nice-to-Haves**
*Definition:* Improvements that enhance usability or experience but do not materially impact trust, adoption, or operational scale.
*Includes:* UI polish and visual refinements; minor workflow optimizations; convenience features and aesthetic improvements.
*Explicitly excludes:* any work that impacts data correctness, security, compliance, or enterprise onboarding success.

## Key Metrics

**Customer and Platform Outcomes** — Time-to-first value for enterprise customers; retention and expansion within data organizations; reduction in critical data reliability incidents.

**Operational Efficiency** — Cost to serve per customer; support tickets per customer and resolution time; mean time to detect and mean time to resolve incidents.

**Governance and Trust** — Audit readiness and policy coverage; governance-related escalations and closure time; customer trust and reliability scores.

## Where RocketShip Is Going

**Near term** — Win enterprise deals by proving trust, reliability, and governance at scale; reduce onboarding friction and operational overhead.

**Mid term** — Become the standard platform enterprises use to operate trusted data products; expand usage across more teams and business-critical workflows.

**Long term** — Own the trusted data operations category, where reliability, governance, and scale are the moat.

## Guiding Principle

RocketShip does not win by shipping the most features. RocketShip wins by making enterprise data trustworthy, governable, and scalable.

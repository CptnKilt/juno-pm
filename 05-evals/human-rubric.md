## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Bug Prioritization Accuracy
- **Value proposition:**

Evaluate whether Juno accurately identifies, consolidates, and prioritizes reported bugs using evidence from Slack, Notion, and Jira while supporting RocketShip's Trust at Scale strategy.

Each individual bug recommendation is evaluated against four criteria:

1. **Bug Identification and Consolidation:** The bug is correctly identified, and duplicate reports are accurately consolidated.
2. **Existing Work Verification:** Juno correctly identifies whether the bug is already being addressed, based on current Jira information.
3. **Prioritization Accuracy:** The recommended priority is justified by customer impact, likelihood of affecting customers, urgency, and the number of independent supporting sources.
4. **Evidence Verification:** All factual findings are supported by verifiable source citations, and missing or conflicting information is clearly identified.

A recommendation is considered accurate only when all four criteria are satisfied. The Senior Product Manager reviews these recommendations during each weekly evaluation.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bug Identification Accuracy | Did Juno correctly identify the reported bugs from the available sources? | Yes / No | Juno misses relevant bugs, includes unrelated items, or reports bugs that are not supported by the sources. | n/a | Juno identifies all relevant reported bugs in the evaluation dataset without including unrelated requests or inventing issues. |
| Duplicate Bug Detection | Did Juno correctly combine duplicate bug reports without merging unrelated issues? | Yes / No | Juno lists the same bug multiple times, incorrectly merges unrelated bugs, or loses supporting evidence. | n/a | Reports describing the same underlying bug are grouped into one issue, with all supporting sources preserved. |
| Existing Work Identification | Did Juno correctly identify and separate bugs already being worked on? | Yes / No | Juno recommends already-active bugs as new work or incorrectly excludes bugs that are not being addressed. | n/a | Juno accurately identifies bugs with active work in Jira and separates them from the list of new fixes requiring prioritization. |
| Prioritization Accuracy | Did Juno prioritize bugs based on customer impact, likelihood, urgency, and independent supporting sources? | Yes / No | Juno overlooks important factors, ranks low-impact issues above critical bugs without justification, or produces unsupported rankings. | n/a | The proposed rankings reflect the four defined factors, are supported by available evidence, and appropriately elevate critical customer-impacting issues. |
| Evidence and Citation Verification | Did Juno provide verifiable source citations and flag missing or conflicting information? | Yes / No | Juno provides missing, incorrect, or fabricated citations, presents unsupported claims as facts, or conceals important evidence gaps | n/a | Every source-derived finding includes a traceable citation, and missing, conflicting, or unverified information is clearly flagged for PM review. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** Sample Size: 10 Juno-generated bug-prioritization reports per week.  Frequency: Weekly, during the initial evaluation period of 8 weeks.  Selection Method: Randomly select 10 completed reports each week, including both successful and partially completed reports when available. Ensure the sample includes reports with different confidence levels and bug priorities.  Each report will be evaluated using the five Yes/No questions in the Human Evaluation Rubric.
- **Who scores them with you:** Engineering Lead or another PM.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** 1. Review the original Slack, Notion, and Jira evidence.

2. Compare the disputed finding against the rubric's Yes/No definitions.

3. Discuss the reasoning and agree on a final score.

4. If agreement cannot be reached, escalate to a designated Product or Engineering leader for adjudication.

5. Document the disagreement, final decision, and reasoning to improve future evaluations.

## Pass bar

5/5: Pass. 4/5: Conditional pass, corrections required.  0–3/5: Fail, improvement required.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Critical Failure: Fabricated citations or unsupported findings presented as facts automatically fail the evaluation.
